Each line counts how many of that year's top 100 women's players were at a club in that league. The WSL went from 19 in 2016 to 42 in 2025. The NWSL peaked at 29 in 2019 and had 18 in 2025.

Rankings for 2018–2025 are the Guardian's annual top 100; 2016–2017 are from The Offside Rule, which ran the list before the Guardian. A player counts for the club listed with her ranking. "Other leagues" groups every league outside the five in this project.
