In 2025, Chelsea (15), Arsenal (14), Barcelona (12), OL Lyonnes (10) held 51 of the world's top 100 players between them. Hover over a bar to see the players.

Clubs are ranked by how many top-100 players they have. Tied clubs share a place (shown as T) and the next place is skipped, so two clubs tied 10th means the next club is 12th. A "Top" view shows every club placed at that rank or better, so ties can show extra clubs. "All" shows clubs placed 19th or better and groups the rest as Other.

Club is the club listed with the ranking. Players at a US college are grouped as University (NCAA).
