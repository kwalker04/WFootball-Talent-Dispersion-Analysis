Who reaches the late stages of the Champions League, 2016–17 to 2025–26

Stage
View

Over the last 10 seasons (2016–17 to 2025–26), the eight top clubs filled 60 of the 80 quarter-final places. From the semi-finals on, nobody else got through: they took all 40 of 40 semi-final places, every place in the final, and every title.

The eight top clubs are the top and secondary teams in the table above: Barcelona, Lyon, PSG, Chelsea, Arsenal, Manchester City, Wolfsburg and Bayern Munich. Lyon and OL Lyonnes are counted as one club. Hover over the Other bar to see which clubs it includes.
