Each row shows how far the average champion finished ahead (red, right) and how far the average last-place team finished behind (navy, left), in goals per game. Leagues are ordered by how dominant their champion is. Under each league: its clubs' Champions League finals and titles, 2016–17 to 2025–26.

The leagues with the most dominant champions are the ones that win in Europe. Spain's gap comes mostly from the top (champion +3.91, last place -1.38); Germany's comes mostly from the bottom (champion +2.99, last place -2.99). With four leagues this is a pattern, not proof.

League tables: averages over every season in team_seasons.csv (2018–19 to 2024–25). Champions League: uwcl_knockout_stages_2016_2026.csv.
