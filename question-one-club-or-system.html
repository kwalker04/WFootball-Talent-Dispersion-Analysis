Shares come from womensT100.csv, with Lyon and OL Lyonnes counted as one club. In Germany, Wolfsburg leads over the full period, but the trend has turned: Bayern won the last four titles, and Wolfsburg's top-100 players fell from 10 in 2022 to 1 in 2025.

Champions League performance

If a few clubs really are too good for everyone else, the Champions League is where it should show. It is the one competition where the best teams from different leagues play each other, and it is open to clubs from all of UEFA's 55 member associations. In 2025/26, 74 teams from 50 different associations entered (UEFA, 2025). Yet very few of them ever get far. The chart below counts which clubs reached each stage over the last ten seasons.
